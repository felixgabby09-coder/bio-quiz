Module 1: Synaptic Pharmacology & Reuptake

Neurotransmitters are released into the synaptic cleft where they bind to postsynaptic receptors. To terminate signalling, reuptake transporters clear neurotransmitters from the synapse back into the presynaptic neuron. Selective Serotonin Reuptake Inhibitors (SSRIs) block these transporters, increasing extracellular serotonin availability.
